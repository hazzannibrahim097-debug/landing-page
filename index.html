FAQs

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

You can shorten a link without an account, but creating one lets you manage, customize and track all your links in one place.

Shortened links stay active for as long as your account is in good standing. Paid plans let you set your own expiry rules.

The Basic plan includes unlimited URL shortening. Higher plans add extra features such as branded domains and advanced analytics.
